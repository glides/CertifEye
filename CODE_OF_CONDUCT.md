# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

This project handles security-sensitive assessment evidence. Contributors, users, maintainers, and issue participants must keep discussions respectful, practical, and focused on defensive improvement.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and patient with people of different experience levels.
- Keep security discussions concrete, reproducible, and defensive.
- Use synthetic, scrubbed, or clearly fictional evidence in public collaboration.
- Never post real secrets, identities, raw exports, salts, token maps, private manifests, reports, or screenshots.
- Follow the security policy for suspected privacy or fail-closed defects.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, personal attacks, or repeated disruptive comments.
- Publishing private information or real environment artifacts.
- Requests for exploitation, credential theft, relay execution, evasion, or unauthorized access.
- Pressuring contributors to review production evidence outside an approved secure process.

Maintainers may remove content, close discussions, or block participants when needed to protect people, environments, and the project.
